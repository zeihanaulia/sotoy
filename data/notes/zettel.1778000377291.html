Effective agent context is a sweet spot, not maximum context

Kualitas review agent menurun jika konteks terlalu banyak dan penuh noise. Sweet spot adalah konteks yang cukup untuk masalah, tanpa overload yang membuat model kehilangan fokus.

“Output quality is a curve against context. Too little, and you get generic slop. Too much, and the model drowns in what it has been fed and the output gets vaguer and more confident at the same time.”
