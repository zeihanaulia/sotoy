AI coding sukses ketika sistem bisa memverifikasi output dengan cara objektif: compile, lint, test, dan run.
Tanpa loop verifikasi ini, model hanya menghasilkan teks yang terlihat seperti kode, bukan kode yang dapat dipercaya.

> “Verified used to mean ‘read by you’. With modern agent throughput, it has to mean ‘checked by tests, by type checkers, by automated gates, or by you where your judgement matters’.”
