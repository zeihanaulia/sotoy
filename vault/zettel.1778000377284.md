Senior engineers paling bernilai ketika mereka memindahkan judgement ke harness: membuat rule, skill, dan gate, bukan hanya membaca diff dan mengomentari.
Jika issue berulang muncul, solusi terbaik bukan komentar manual tapi automasi review dan pencegahan.

> “The senior engineer’s job is to train the AI, not review its output.”
